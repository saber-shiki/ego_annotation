- Left hand contact/occlusion remains unresolved. The visible evidence in 690–725 does not expose enough left hand surface; the needed mechanism must infer or carry uncertainty through occlusion/contact rather than fit forearm pixels.
- The current interval solver can optimize MANO root translation, root/wrist orientation, and articulation from a replayable source, but it lacks a trustworthy per-vertex hand-owned visible-surface/occlusion/contact observation family. Visible-surface depth-order rows select zero MANO vertices in the calibrated branch.
- HOT3D now scores 2D hand boxes and 3D MANO hand localization/articulation on the fixed three-clip pinhole slice. It still does not score contact, occlusion ownership, nonpenetration, object pose, or full V19 physical pipeline performance, because those require V19 object/contact state on HOT3D clips and a declared GT comparison adapter for those variables.
- H2O and DexYCB adapters remain unimplemented. They should not be opened unless HOT3D is blocked or after a stronger HOT3D physical-state adapter exposes a metric claim family that HOT3D cannot cover.
- Renderer/state naming still uses extracted `v18_*` components. This is acceptable only while they consume V19-produced inputs and the final render honestly shows uncertainty.
