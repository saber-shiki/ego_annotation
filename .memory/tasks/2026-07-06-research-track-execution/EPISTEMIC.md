# Current epistemic model

The research task is a mechanism-resolution problem. The artifact that matters is renderable HOI annotation whose visible state is driven by measured geometry, pose, contact, occlusion, and hand-correction rows. A row or graph-health decision is useful only if it changes the contact/geometry state a renderer consumes.

The first concrete slice is HOT3D clip001850 keyboard, right hand, frames 28-48 in `/data2/ego_annotation_outputs/v19_runs/20260626_hot3d_clip001850_pinhole_a800_native_v5_focalfix_coordrigid_v1`.

Current contact conclusion for this slice: GT-free right-hand keyboard contact is **unresolved with zero admissible contact frames**. The prior route “wire interval observed-surface penetration into contact/NP” is rejected. The interval MANO penetration channel is a signed query against the inflated completed mesh filtered to depth-supported faces. Keyboard-masked, hand-quarantined depth refutes it on mask-available frames: f32 has zero eligible penetrating vertices and the hand is 144 mm in front of keyboard depth while the interval channel reports 82 mm mesh penetration; f36 has zero eligible penetrating vertices and is 81 mm in front while the interval channel reports 107 mm mesh penetration and a small full-frame non-keyboard leak.

Object geometry mechanism: the completed TRELLIS keyboard body is the source of the phantom. It is non-watertight, 95.4% inferred hidden surface, free-space-uncarved, and about 8.4x too thick in its thin axis. Observed-only body repair extracts 3221 observed faces and removes the TRELLIS completion from the contact body. Free-space carving removes 49,222 of 87,671 carveable faces, but the body remains open because the back/sides/bottom are never observed. This repair invalidates false penetration and improves the rendered body/provenance, but it does not create contact: observed-only unsigned distances remain 35.6-115.2 mm and keyboard depth gaps remain 74.7-179.3 mm.

Hand-depth mechanism: the contact-forcing hand-depth counterfactual is refuted. To move fingertips to the keyboard-masked surface requires frame-varying along-ray shifts of 83-202 mm (median 134 mm), far beyond the 25-30 mm systematic metric floor, and produces catastrophic 2D reprojection displacement (62-169 px median, 110-285 px max). Only f36 reaches a contact_candidate after such a shift, and even that breaks projection. Therefore an ~8 cm hand-depth correction is not a physically admissible route to contact.

Canonical `ego.hoi.contact_frame_detail/0.1.0` carries the honest per-frame states for the kill-test window: `pose_unresolved` on 28,29,37-44,47,48; `geometry_epoch_contaminated` on 30-35 and 46; `full_frame_depth_leak` on 36; `unresolved_incoherent_evidence` on 45. f45 was demoted from the kill-test script’s permissive `contact_candidate_keyboard_masked` because it is one thumb vertex at +5.86 mm on a near-depth outlier, with no compact temporally persistent patch.

Render-consumer mechanism: the published clip001850 videos were produced by a Stage-1 renderer that baked in the raw TRELLIS body, zero-by-construction `penverts=0`, and a bare `UNCERTAIN` state, then Stage-2 `publish_v19_render_artifact.py` stamped one static banner on every frame. Stage-2 cannot express per-frame contact states. A three-frame still consumer is QC evidence only. The artifact-changing consumer is the full-duration replacement render at `/data2/ego_annotation_outputs/research_clip001850_contact_state_20260706/v19_contact_state_full_duration/`: 150 frames at 30 fps, canonical-named `v19_overlay.mp4`, `v19_world.mp4`, and `v19_side_by_side.mp4`, driven by the durable contact table and the durable repaired observed body. It defaults the 129 frames outside the contact table to `unresolved_evidence_incomplete`, renders f32/f36/f45 as `geometry_epoch_contaminated` / `full_frame_depth_leak` / `unresolved_incoherent_evidence`, draws the 3221-face observed body hash `6d4bcebfb76149a4...` as amber/open/hatched rather than the solid TRELLIS mesh, and removes the false `gap 39.2mm`, `penverts=0`, and bare `UNCERTAIN` claims from the replacement pixels/manifest. A generic parameterized consumer now reproduces the same artifact-level gates from durable inputs and enforces floor-independent provenance for any future `confirmed_contact` row.

Sidecar mechanism: the slice is packaged as an `ego.hoi` sidecar under `/data2/ego_annotation_outputs/research_clip001850_contact_state_20260706/ego_hoi_sidecar/extensions/org.ego.hoi/0.1.0/clip001850_research/`. It has 150 `contact_frame_detail` rows, geometry/body provenance, render-consumption rows pointing to the full-duration videos and their hashes, graph-health provenance, and a motion-coupling channel table. The sidecar is useful because it points to the rendered artifact and byte-reproduces f32/f36/f45 from the table; it does not add new contact evidence.

Motion-coupling mechanism: the only admissible GT-free contact-promotion channel was tested and is negative on clip001850. Direct keyboard pose edges have a motion floor of 76.9 mm translation / 18.2° rotation. The fingertip approach peak at f36 has keyboard response only 8.6 mm, below the 20.2 mm window direct-edge median; Spearman object-motion vs approach speed is −0.25 (p=0.59); the largest object edge f46 is a withdrawal-time mask-reacquire/refit jump, not a hand impulse; closest approach f39 is interpolated and has no independent object measurement. Therefore no frame in f28-48 can truthfully populate `motion_coupling_onset`, and `confirmed_contact` remains unreachable GT-free. The only remaining adjudicator for this slice is HOT3D GT/R8, whose evaluator code exists but whose GT bundle is not on local disk.

Current frontier mechanism: static-held object pose with visibility ledger. The P15 keyboard pose graph is inert by construction: `solve_v19_rigid_object_pose_graph.py` optimizes correction deltas around raw ICP fits, but all non-nonpenetration residuals are homogeneous in the correction field, P15 ran with `constraint_report=null`, and the available P16 nonpenetration state has zero accepted correction targets. Thus `x0=0` is the optimum (`nfev=1`, `cost=0`) and the 13 direct ICP poses pass through unchanged. KT-L1 classifies the physical regime from pose-independent observed-surface evidence: the keyboard is static, not moving; the rest cluster is f30-36 and f46 with centroid `[0.599,-0.094,0.339] m`, internal spread 21.6 mm, and no moving-observed frames. f60/f75/f76/f77 are mask drift/outliers; f75 is the contaminated visible-geometry anchor itself, 199.5 mm from rest, so the upstream eligibility gate and canonical seed are anchored to a bad frame. f45 remains unresolved/hand-occluded. KT-L2 refutes support recovery from existing masks: all 72 f78-149 visible-surface-ineligible frames are mask-drift delaminated, hand/depth-cleaned centroids remain 138-278 mm from rest (median 166 mm), 0/72 recover consensus support, and direct support cannot truthfully rise above 13/150 from this run. The accepted local intervention is therefore a robust static `T_rest` estimated from the rest cluster, outlier rejection for f60/f75/f76/f77, covariance/observability from the cluster, and a per-frame visibility ledger (`static_observed`, `unresolved_hand_occluded`, `mask_unreliable`, `occluded_out_of_view`) consumed by the renderer. The rendered body on f78-149 must stop using the f77 outlier as an opaque held pose; it should move to the rest gauge with uncertainty styling or be explicitly occluded/mask-unreliable. A smoothed trajectory, mask quarantine, or graph-health-only row is now ruled out.
