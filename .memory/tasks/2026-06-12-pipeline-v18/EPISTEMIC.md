## Qualitative-vs-quantitative judgment rule

The live distinction is not “above/below a residual threshold.” Ordinary quantitative error means the mechanism is qualitatively coherent and the remaining discrepancy is plausibly measurement noise, model noise, limited observability, or uncertainty already represented in the artifact. A qualitative pipeline mistake means the dataflow or physical mechanism is wrong: the rendered hand/object relation is impossible, a hidden/free-space-conflicted surface is used as a hard constraint, an object mask owns hand pixels, a contact label is not caused by physical variables, a case branch substitutes for perception, or the final render contradicts the claimed state. This distinction must be made by inspecting rendered overlays/world geometry/depth ownership/temporal behavior and mechanism provenance. Scalars can guide attention but cannot decide the category.
